Place favicon files here so they are served at the site root:

  favicon.ico          →  /favicon.ico
  favicon-16x16.png  →  /favicon-16x16.png  (16×16)
  favicon-32x32.png  →  /favicon-32x32.png  (32×32)
  apple-touch-icon.png → /apple-touch-icon.png (optional, iOS / PWA)

The app links to these in every page head. Legacy URLs /favicon16.png and
/favicon32.png redirect to the hyphenated PNG names.
